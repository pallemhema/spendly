Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Spendly is a personal expense tracking tool, and we are committed to handling your data responsibly and transparently.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, email address, and a password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text).

As you use Spendly, we collect the expense data you choose to record — including the amount, category, date, and any description you add. We also store basic technical information such as the timestamps of your activity, solely to operate and secure the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide only to deliver and improve Spendly. Specifically, we use it to authenticate your account, store and display your transactions, generate summaries and category breakdowns, and respond to support requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles of any kind.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is associated with your account only. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

You may update or correct your personal information at any time through the application, and you may delete individual expenses or your entire account by contacting us. When you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by law.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small set of trusted third-party services to operate — for example, hosting providers that store the database, and (where applicable) email services that deliver transactional messages such as account verification.

These providers receive only the information needed to perform their function and are expected to handle it securely. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, want to request access to or deletion of your data, or have a concern about how your information is handled, please reach out to us through the contact information provided in the application.

We will respond to privacy-related requests in a timely and transparent manner.
